🔤 Section 1: Double Letter Typos (Most Common)

accomodate→accommodate
Fix: Double 'c' and double 'm' - ac-com-mo-date.
comittee→committee
Fix: Double 'm' and double 't' - com-mit-tee.
embarass→embarrass
Fix: Double 'r' and double 's' - em-bar-rass.
occured→occurred
Fix: Double 'c' and double 'r' - oc-cur-red.
neccessary→necessary
Fix: One 'c', two 's' - ne-ces-sary. Mnemonic: "one collar, two sleeves"
succesful→successful
Fix: Double 'c' and double 's' - suc-cess-ful.
reccomend→recommend
Fix: One 'c', two 'm' - re-com-mend.
adress→address
Fix: Double 'd' and double 's' - ad-dress.
agressive→aggressive
Fix: Double 'g' and double 's' - ag-gres-sive.
tommorow→tomorrow
Fix: One 'm', two 'r' - to-mor-row. Mnemonic: "Tom or row"

🔡 Section 2: Vowel Confusion Typos

definately→definitely
Fix: 'a' should be 'i' - de-fi-nite-ly. Mnemonic: "finite" is inside "definitely"
seperate→separate
Fix: 'e' should be 'a' - sep-a-rate. Mnemonic: "there is 'a rat' in separate"
recieve→receive
Fix: 'ei' not 'ie' - re-ceive. Rule: 'i' before 'e' except after 'c'
wierd→weird
Fix: 'ie' should be 'ei' - weird. This is an exception.
acheive→achieve
Fix: 'ei' should be 'ie' - a-chieve.
beleive→believe
Fix: 'ei' should be 'ie' - be-lieve. "Never believe a lie"
foriegn→foreign
Fix: 'ie' should be 'ei' - foreign.
concieve→conceive
Fix: 'ei' after 'c' - con-ceive.
decieve→deceive
Fix: 'ei' after 'c' - de-ceive.
percieve→perceive
Fix: 'ei' after 'c' - per-ceive.

🤫 Section 3: Silent Letter Typos

wednesday→Wednesday
Fix: Silent 'd' - Wed-nes-day.
restraunt→restaurant
Fix: 'au' not 'au'? It is 'au' - res-tau-rant.
Febuary→February
Fix: Do not forget the first 'r' - Feb-ru-ary.
calender→calendar
Fix: 'er' should be 'ar' - ca-len-dar.
lightening→lightning
Fix: Remove the extra 'e' - light-ning.
goverment→government
Fix: Do not forget the 'n' - gov-ern-ment.
environment→environment
Fix: Include the 'n' - en-vi-ron-ment.
sofware→software
Fix: Include the 't' - soft + ware.

🔉 Section 4: Homophone Typos (Same Sound, Different Spelling)

there→their / they're
Fix: There = place, Their = possession, They're = they are.
your→you're
Fix: Your = possession, You're = you are.
its→it's
Fix: Its = possession, It's = it is.
to→too / two
Fix: To = direction, Too = also/excessive, Two = number 2.
affect→effect
Fix: Affect = verb, Effect = noun.
then→than
Fix: Then = time, Than = comparison.
loose→lose
Fix: Loose = not tight, Lose = misplace.

🔄 Section 5: Transposition Typos (Letter Order Mix-ups)

teh→the
Fix: The most common transposition typo. Slow down on common words!
form→from
Fix: 'r' and 'o' are swapped. "FROM has R before O"
woudl→would
Fix: 'u' and 'l' are swapped. "WOULD has U before L"
adn→and
Fix: 'n' and 'd' are swapped. "AND has N before D"
thsi→this
Fix: 'i' and 's' are swapped.

📝 Section 6: Other Common Typos

alot→a lot
Fix: Two words - a lot.
untill→until
Fix: One 'l' - un-til.
with out→without
Fix: One word - without.
can not→cannot
Fix: Usually one word - cannot.
in to→into
Fix: One word when indicating movement.
priviledge→privilege
Fix: No 'd' - priv-i-lege.
publick→public
Fix: No 'k' at the end - pub-lic.
concious→conscious
Fix: 's' after 'c' - con-scious.
apparant→apparent
Fix: 'e' after 'r' - ap-par-ent.
independant→independent
Fix: 'a' should be 'e' - in-de-pen-dent.
