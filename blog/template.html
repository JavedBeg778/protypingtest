1. Master Touch Typing

Touch typing is the foundation of fast typing. It means typing without looking at the keyboard, using all ten fingers. When you look at the keyboard, your speed is limited to 30-40 WPM. With touch typing, you can achieve 60-100+ WPM.

To master touch typing, start by placing your fingers on the home row: left hand on A S D F and right hand on J K L ;. Your thumbs should rest on the space bar. The F and J keys have small bumps to help you find the home row without looking.

💡 Pro Tip by Pro Typing Test: Practice home row exercises for 15 minutes daily. Type "asdf jkl;" repeatedly until it becomes automatic. Then gradually add top row (qwert yuiop) and bottom row (zxcv bn m).

2. Focus on Accuracy First

Many beginners make the mistake of prioritizing speed over accuracy. This is counterproductive. High speed with low accuracy is worthless. Every time you make an error and hit backspace, you lose 3-5 seconds. At 40 WPM, that is 8-10 words lost.

The formula to remember: Effective WPM = Gross WPM × (Accuracy ÷ 100). For example:

  • 50 WPM with 85% accuracy: 42.5 effective WPM (too many errors)
  • 40 WPM with 98% accuracy: 39.2 effective WPM (better for exams)
  • 45 WPM with 95% accuracy: 42.75 effective WPM (ideal)

Always prioritize achieving 95%+ accuracy before increasing your speed. Use Pro Typing Test's accuracy tracking to monitor your progress.

3. Practice Daily with Timed Tests

Consistency is the most important factor in increasing typing speed. Practicing 20-30 minutes daily is far more effective than practicing 2 hours once a week. Your brain needs daily reinforcement to build muscle memory.

Pro Typing Test offers timed tests of various durations:

  • 10-minute tests: Best for exam preparation and professional typing
💡 Pro Tip by Pro Typing Test: Start each practice session with a 1-minute test to warm up. Then take 2-3 five-minute tests. End with a 10-minute test once a week to build endurance.

4. Learn Proper Finger Placement

Each finger has specific keys it should press. Using the wrong finger for a key creates awkward hand movements that limit your speed. Here is the correct finger assignment:

  • Left Pinky: Q, A, Z, 1, `, Tab, Caps Lock, Left Shift
  • Left Ring Finger: W, S, X, 2
  • Left Middle Finger: E, D, C, 3
  • Left Index Finger: R, F, V, 4, G, T, B, 5
  • Right Index Finger: J, U, M, 6, H, Y, N, 7
  • Right Middle Finger: K, I, , (comma), 8
  • Right Ring Finger: L, O, . (period), 9
  • Right Pinky: ;, P, /, 0, [, ], -, =, Backspace, Enter, Right Shift
  • Thumbs: Space bar

For a detailed guide with diagrams, see our Complete Finger Placement Guide.

5. Practice Common Words and Phrases

The 100 most common English words make up 50% of all typing. Mastering these words will dramatically increase your typing speed. The top 20 common words are:

the, be, to, of, and, a, in, that, have, I, it, for, not, on, with, he, as, you, do, at

Practice these words in phrases and sentences. For example: "I have to go to the store and buy some food." Type such sentences repeatedly until they become automatic.
