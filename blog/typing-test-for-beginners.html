1 Understand What is a Typing Test

A typing test measures two things: speed (WPM) and accuracy (%). When you take a test on Pro Typing Test, you will type a passage for a fixed time (1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes). After the test, you will see:

  • Gross WPM: Total words typed per minute (including errors)
  • Net WPM: Total words minus errors × time penalty
  • Accuracy Percentage: (Correct keystrokes ÷ Total keystrokes) × 100
  • Time: Your test duration
🎯 Beginners should focus on NET WPM and Accuracy. It is better to type 15 WPM with 98% accuracy than 25 WPM with 85% accuracy. The first is real skill. The second is rushing with bad habits.

2 Master the Home Row (The Foundation)

The home row is where your fingers rest when not typing. Learn this first. Practice until you can find these keys WITHOUT looking.

A S D F     J K L ;

Left Hand: A (pinky), S (ring), D (middle), F (index)

Right Hand: J (index), K (middle), L (ring), ; (pinky)

Both Thumbs: Space bar

💡 The F and J keys have small bumps - feel for them without looking!

Daily Exercise (Week 1 only):

  • Type asdf jkl; asdf jkl; asdf jkl; for 5 minutes
  • Type fdsa ;lkj fdsa ;lkj fdsa ;lkj for 5 minutes (reverse)
  • Combine: asdf jkl; fdsa ;lkj asdf jkl; fdsa ;lkj

Goal: Type home row keys at 15-20 WPM with 98% accuracy before moving to the next step.

3 Learn Proper Finger Placement

Each finger has assigned keys. This is NOT optional. Using all 10 fingers is what separates 30 WPM typists from 70 WPM typists.

Left Hand Finger Assignment:

  • Pinky: A, Q, Z, 1, `, Tab, Caps Lock, Left Shift
  • Ring: S, W, X, 2
  • Middle: D, E, C, 3
  • Index: F, R, V, 4, G, T, B, 5 (covers 6 keys!)

Right Hand Finger Assignment:

  • Index: J, U, M, 6, H, Y, N, 7 (covers 6 keys!)
  • Middle: K, I, , (comma), 8
  • Ring: L, O, . (period), 9
  • Pinky: ;, P, /, 0, -, =, [, ], \, Right Shift, Enter
🖐️ Beginner's Secret: Start with the home row keys only for the first 2 weeks. Then add the top row (QWERTYUIOP). Then the bottom row (ZXCVBNM). Do not rush - each row takes time to feel natural.

5 Focus on Accuracy First, Speed Second

This is the most important lesson in this guide. Accuracy is the foundation of speed. You cannot build speed on bad habits.

The Accuracy Math:

Typist A: 30 WPM with 85% accuracy = Effective 25.5 WPM

Typist B: 25 WPM with 98% accuracy = Effective 24.5 WPM

Result: They are almost the same! But Typist B has better habits and will reach 50 WPM faster.

Your Beginner Accuracy Targets:

  • Week 1-2: 92-94% accuracy (allow some mistakes - you are learning)
  • Week 3-4: 95%+ accuracy (consistent now)
  • Month 2 onward: 96-98% accuracy (professional level)

Pro Tip: If your accuracy drops below 92%, SLOW DOWN. You are rushing. Type at a comfortable speed where errors are rare.

6 Practice with Simple Words & Sentences

Do not jump into complex paragraphs. Start with the 100 most common English words. Master these first.

Top 30 Most Common Words (Start Here):

the, be, to, of, and, a, in, that, have, I, it, for, not, on, with, he, as, you, do, at, this, but, his, by, from, they, we, say, her, she

Practice Method:

  • Type each word 10 times - focus on accuracy
  • Then create short sentences: "I have a pen. You have a book."
  • When comfortable, move to beginner typing paragraphs

Resource: Bigram Blitz - practice common two-letter combinations like "th", "he", "in", "er", "an".

10 Set Realistic Weekly Goals

Do not aim for 60 WPM in your first month. That is unrealistic and leads to frustration. Set small, achievable goals:

  • Month 1 Goal: 20-25 WPM with 95%+ accuracy
  • Month 2 Goal: 25-30 WPM with 95%+ accuracy
  • Month 3 Goal: 30-35 WPM with 95%+ accuracy
  • Months 4-6 Goal: 35-45 WPM with 95%+ accuracy
🎯 Remember: Even 30 WPM is useful for most jobs! Many administrative roles only require 35 WPM. You do not need to be a 100 WPM typist to succeed. Progress, not perfection.

📅 30-Day Beginner Action Plan

Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

  • Days 1-3: ONLY home row practice. Type "asdf jkl;" for 15 minutes daily. No other keys.
  • Days 4-5: Add top row (qwert yuiop) - practice 15 minutes
  • Days 6-7: Add bottom row (zxcv bn m) - practice 15 minutes
  • Daily: Take 3-5 rounds of 1-minute tests (record scores)
  • Target: 12-15 WPM, 90%+ accuracy

Week 2: Building (Days 8-14)

  • Practice all rows combined - 15 minutes daily
  • Learn 50 most common words - 10 minutes daily
  • Take 5 rounds of 1-minute tests + 2 rounds of 2-minute tests
  • Target: 15-20 WPM, 93%+ accuracy

Week 3: Speed Focus (Days 15-21)

  • Practice short sentences - 10 minutes daily
  • Use Lessons 5-6 (common words + sentences)
  • Take 1-minute, 2-minute, and 5-minute tests
  • Identify weak keys using our Tricky Keys tool
  • Target: 20-25 WPM, 94%+ accuracy

Week 4: Endurance (Days 22-30)

  • Practice full paragraphs from practice library
  • Take 1x 5-minute test + 1x 10-minute test daily
  • Review progress - compare with Day 1 results
  • Plan next month's focus areas
  • Target: 22-28 WPM, 95%+ accuracy
  • 🎓 Day 30: Claim your beginner certificate!
💡 Coach's Note: Do not compare your Week 4 to someone else's Week 20. Everyone learns at their own pace. Focus on YOUR improvement. Even a 5 WPM gain is progress worth celebrating!
