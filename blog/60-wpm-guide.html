📅 Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7) - Touch Typing Basics

Days 1-3: Home Row Mastery

Practice home row keys: asdf jkl; asdf jkl; - 20 minutes daily.
Focus on proper finger placement without looking at the keyboard.

Target: 20 WPM with 95% accuracy

Days 4-5: Top Row Introduction

Add top row: qwert yuiop qwert yuiop - 25 minutes daily.
Combine with home row: asdf jkl; qwert yuiop asdf jkl;

Target: 22 WPM with 95% accuracy

Days 6-7: Bottom Row Introduction

Add bottom row: zxcv bn m zxcv bn m - 25 minutes daily.
Practice all rows combined: "the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog"

Target: 25 WPM with 94% accuracy
💡 Week 1 Tip: This is your foundation week. Mastering correct finger placement is critical. To achieve 60 WPM, you must internalize correct finger placement until it becomes automatic.

⚠️ 7 Common Mistakes That Keep You Stuck Below 60 WPM

❌ 1. Looking at the Keyboard

This is the number one speed killer. Cover your hands with a cloth if necessary.

❌ 2. Sacrificing Accuracy for Speed

Mistakes cost more time than slow, accurate typing.

❌ 3. Inconsistent Practice

30 minutes daily is far more effective than 3 hours once a week.

❌ 4. Wrong Finger Placement

Each finger has assigned keys. Using incorrect fingers creates awkward hand movements.

❌ 5. Poor Posture

Slouching restricts blood flow and breathing.

❌ 6. Only Taking 1-Minute Tests

1-minute tests can inflate your perceived speed.

❌ 7. Not Tracking Progress

What gets measured gets improved.

💡 Important Tips from My Journey (25 WPM → 85 WPM)

💪 Consistency is Key: I never missed a day for 6 months. Do not skip daily practice.
  • Do not look at the keyboard: Cover your hands with a cloth if you must.
  • Accuracy first, speed second: Prioritize accuracy (95%+) — speed will follow.
  • Maintain proper posture: Straight back, elbows at 90°, wrists straight.
  • Take regular breaks: After every 25 minutes of practice, take a 5-minute break.
  • Use all 10 fingers: Utilize all ten fingers; this is the essence of touch typing.
  • Track your progress: I maintained a daily log.
