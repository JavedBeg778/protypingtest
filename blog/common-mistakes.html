❌ Mistake 1: Looking at the Keyboard

Constantly looking at the keyboard is the most common mistake. According to Pro Typing Test data, 70% of beginners make this mistake.

✅ Fix: Practice touch typing. Cover your keyboard with a cloth. Start with home row practice. Use our Touch Typing Guide.
❌ Mistake 2: Wrong Finger Placement

Pressing keys with the wrong fingers reduces accuracy and limits your maximum speed.

✅ Fix: Place your fingers on the home row (ASDF JKL;). Follow our Finger Placement Guide.
❌ Mistake 3: Rushing Too Much

Typing too quickly leads to increased errors. Each error wastes 3-5 seconds on corrections.

✅ Fix: Slow down. Prioritize accuracy first (95%+), then build speed.
❌ Mistake 4: Bad Posture

Slouching while sitting and resting your wrists on the keyboard causes fatigue and errors.

✅ Fix: Keep your back straight, elbows at 90°, wrists straight and floating.
❌ Mistake 5: Not Using All Fingers

Using only 2-4 fingers severely limits your speed. Maximum speed with 2 fingers is 40-50 WPM.

✅ Fix: Use all 10 fingers. Start with Typing Lessons that teach proper finger assignment.
❌ Mistake 6: Ignoring Accuracy

Focusing only on speed while ignoring accuracy is a critical error. Most tests have 95% accuracy requirements.

✅ Fix: Effective WPM = Gross WPM × Accuracy. Set a target of 95%+ accuracy.
❌ Mistake 7: Inconsistent Practice

Practicing inconsistently prevents muscle memory development. Typing is a physical skill requiring daily practice.

✅ Fix: Practice 20-30 minutes daily. Even 15 minutes daily is better than 2 hours weekly.
