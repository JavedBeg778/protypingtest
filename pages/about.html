💬 Recommended Practice Approach

Consistency Over Volume
Practicing 15 to 20 minutes daily yields substantially greater muscle memory than a single 2-hour cram session once a week.
Practice Tip 1
Prioritize Accuracy
Keep your accuracy above 95%. Fixing typing errors mid-stream slows typing down far more than typing at a steady, rhythmic pace.
Practice Tip 2
Check Posture & Ergonomics
Position your elbows at 90 degrees, keep wrists hover-straight, and take 30-second breaks between 10-minute typing tests.
