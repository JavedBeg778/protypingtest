What is WPM in typing?

+
WPM stands for Words Per Minute. It's the standard measure of typing speed, calculated by counting how many words you can type in one minute. A "word" is standardized as 5 characters, including spaces and punctuation. Learn more in our What is WPM guide.

How is typing speed calculated?

+
Typing speed formula: WPM = (Total characters typed / 5) / Time in minutes. We also factor in errors - incorrect words reduce your effective speed. For example, if you type 200 characters in 1 minute with 2 errors, your Net WPM would be around 38. Try our 1-minute test to check your speed!

What is a good typing speed?

+
  • Average: 40 WPM
  • Good: 50-60 WPM
  • Professional: 70-80 WPM
  • Expert: 90+ WPM
For government exams in India, requirement is usually 30-40 WPM depending on the position. Check our exam requirements guide for detailed information.

What's the difference between Gross WPM and Net WPM?

+
Gross WPM is your raw typing speed without considering errors. Net WPM subtracts errors from your score: Net WPM = Gross WPM - (Errors / Time). This gives a more accurate measure of your actual typing ability. We always show Net WPM in our tests because it reflects real-world performance.

How can I improve my typing speed?

+
  • Practice daily for 15-20 minutes
  • Use proper finger placement (touch typing)
  • Focus on accuracy first, then speed
  • Take regular breaks to avoid fatigue
  • Use our structured lessons
  • Check our 60 WPM guide for a structured plan
Consistency is key! Even 15 minutes daily shows significant improvement within 4-6 weeks.
