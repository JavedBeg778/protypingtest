❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a typing benchmark?
A typing benchmark is a standardized test that measures your typing speed (WPM) and accuracy, then compares your score against global averages to determine your skill level from Beginner to Elite.
What is the average typing speed?
The average typing speed is 40 WPM. Only 7% of typists reach Professional level (61-80 WPM), and just 1% achieve Elite status (100+ WPM).
Is the typing benchmark free?
Yes! The typing benchmark test is completely free on Pro Typing Test. No registration required.
How can I improve my benchmark score?
Practice daily with our typing lessons, focus on accuracy first, use the Tricky Keys tool for problem letters, and take benchmark tests regularly to track your progress.
