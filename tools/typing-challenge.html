❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a typing challenge?
Simply browse the active challenges below and click 'Join Challenge' on any challenge that interests you. The test will start immediately when you begin typing.
How are challenge scores tracked?
Your challenge scores are saved locally in your browser so you can monitor your personal bests over time. No registration or account is required.
How often are new challenges added?
New daily challenges are added every day at midnight UTC. Weekly challenges refresh every Monday, and monthly challenges start on the 1st of each month.
Can I participate in multiple challenges at once?
Yes! You can join as many active challenges as you want. Your best score from each challenge will count toward the leaderboard.
