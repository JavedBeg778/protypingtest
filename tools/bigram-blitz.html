❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are bigrams in typing?
Bigrams are two-letter combinations that appear frequently in English text, such as 'th', 'er', 'an', and 're'. Mastering these bigrams builds muscle memory and can increase your typing speed by 10-20 WPM.
How can bigram practice improve my typing speed?
The 20 most common bigrams make up over 50% of all typed English text. By practicing these letter pairs, you develop automatic finger movements that directly translate to faster overall typing speed.
