Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Is the typing certificate really free?
Yes! Pro Typing Test offers completely free typing certificates. No hidden charges, no subscription fees, no credit card required. Just pass the typing test and download your certificate instantly.
🔹 How long is the certificate valid?
Your earned certificate is saved directly in your browser localStorage and can be re-downloaded anytime from this device, or shared via your unique verification link.
🔹 Can employers verify my certificate?
Yes! Yes. When you share your verification link, anyone can visit our verification page to confirm the candidate name, test date, level, WPM, and accuracy recorded during the test.
🔹 What WPM is required for jobs in USA and UK?
Most professional jobs require 50-60 WPM for administrative roles, 60-70 WPM for data entry, and 70-80 WPM for executive assistant positions.

Use our free resources: typing lessons, practice paragraphs, typing games, and error analysis tools. Most users achieve their target within 4-8 weeks of daily practice.

Absolutely! You can retake the test as many times as you want, completely free. There's no limit.

Certification Evaluation Methodology

Standard Word Units: Every 5 keystrokes (including spaces and punctuation) count as 1 standard word, ensuring strict parity with international typing speed benchmarks.
Metric Standard: CPM / 5
Real-Time Verification: Net speed deducts uncorrected errors, requiring disciplined accuracy alongside raw typing pace before issuing any milestone credential.
Scoring: Net WPM & Accuracy
Tamper-Resistant Validation: Each completed certificate receives an HMAC-SHA256 signature generated by serverless functions, preventing local score falsification.
Security: Server-Side HMAC
